## <span style="color: #ff6b35;">Why QuietBan?</span>

The Blizzard ignore list fills up fast. QuietBan keeps its own list, with no limit and no setup. Install it and ban.

## <span style="color: #0cf;">Quick start</span>

1. Install QuietBan and start the game. Your Blizzard ignore list is imported once, on first load.
2. Right-click a player (portrait, chat name, group, raid), then <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Ban player</strong></span>.
3. Type <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>/qb list</strong></span>, or click QuietBan in the minimap addon menu, to see your bans, search them and unban in one click. Right-clicking a banned player offers <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Unban</strong></span> too.

## <span style="color: #0cf;">What a ban does</span>

- <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Chat hidden:</strong></span> say, yell, whispers, emotes, channels, party, raid, raid warnings, instance, and the "invited you to a channel" line.
- <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Declined without a popup or a sound:</strong></span> group invites, guild invites, channel invites, trades and duels.
- <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Unlimited list,</strong></span> saved for your whole account.
- <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>11 languages:</strong></span> enUS, frFR, deDE, esES, esMX, itIT, ptBR, ruRU, koKR, zhCN, zhTW.

## <span style="color: #0cf;">Commands</span>

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/qb ban <name>` | Ban a player. No name: your target |
| `/qb unban <name>` | Remove a ban |
| `/qb list` | Bans window, with a search box and an Unban button on each line |

`/quietban` works too.

## <span style="color: #0cf;">Limits</span>

- <span style="color: #ffd100;"><strong>Chat lockdown:</strong></span> while the game locks chat for addons, lines pass unfiltered. Accepting a channel invite during that time can break the channel lines of that chat window until `/reload`.
- Built for WoW Forever.

## <span style="color: #3c3;">Fair play</span>

QuietBan only hides and declines, on your own client. It sends no message to other players and collects no data. Free and open source.
