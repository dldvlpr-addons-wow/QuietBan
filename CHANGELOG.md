# QuietBan

## 1.1.0

### Features
- **Quiet mode**: `/qb quiet` declines group, guild and channel invites, trades and duels from everyone, without a popup or a sound. `/qb quiet on` and `/qb quiet off` work too. Off by default.
- **Choose what quiet mode declines**: `/qb quiet party`, `guild`, `channel`, `trade` or `duel` turns one kind on or off. Trades you start yourself are never declined.
- **Channel invite line** hidden in quiet mode.

## 1.0.0

### Features
- **Ban players**: `/qb ban <name>`, or right-click a player, then **Ban player**. Right-click a banned player to **Unban**.
- **Any letter case**: `/qb ban` matches accented and Cyrillic names however they are typed.
- **Chat hidden**: say, yell, whispers, emotes, channels, party, raid, raid warnings, instance, and the channel invite line.
- **Declined without a popup or a sound**: group, guild and channel invites, trades and duels. Channel invites no longer flash on screen.
- **Bans window**: `/qb list` or the minimap addon menu, with a search box and an Unban button on each line.
- **Blizzard ignore list** imported once, on first load.
- **Unlimited list**, saved for the whole account.
- **11 languages**: enUS, frFR, deDE, esES, esMX, itIT, ptBR, ruRU, koKR, zhCN, zhTW.
